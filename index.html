The fleet

Two vehicle classes built for daily commercial use.

Pick the mix your routes need. Start with a handful of vehicles and add more as you grow.

2W

Electric 2-wheelers

For last-mile delivery, quick commerce, food and parcel riders.

  • Rider-ready commercial scooters
  • Ideal for high-frequency, short-hop routes
  • Flexible fleet sizes for peak and off-peak
3W

Electric 3-wheelers

For cargo, mid-mile logistics and higher-payload distribution.

  • Cargo configurations for bulk deliveries
  • Suited to warehouse-to-store and B2B runs
  • Lower running cost than ICE equivalents
